## The question

Describe the specific problem or question in plain language. Give readers enough context to understand why it mattered. Name the audience or setting without sharing private information.

## My contribution

State what you did yourself and what others did. If this was a course exercise, team assignment, or personal exploration, say so. Link to an artifact you have permission to share.

## The approach

Explain the two or three decisions that most affected your work. Describe why you chose them and which alternatives you considered. Define unfamiliar terms for a reader outside your field.

## What happened

Report the actual outcome. Separate observations from your interpretation. An incomplete, negative, or inconclusive result can still support a useful story when you explain it honestly. Include numbers only when you can explain where they came from.

## What I learned

Describe one idea you understand better now and one thing you would change next time. Connect the lesson to a specific event in the project.

## Evidence and next steps

Add links to supporting material, with descriptive link text. Credit collaborators and sources. Explain what remains unfinished or uncertain, and label future plans as plans.
