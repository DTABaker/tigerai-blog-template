Use this template for a thoughtful account of a course topic, workshop, reading, or skill you practiced. No experience is implied by this placeholder. Replace the sample date with the date you choose to publish.

## Where I started

Describe what you knew before the experience and what you hoped to understand. Give the real context, such as a class, independent study, or club activity.

## What challenged me

Explain one specific obstacle or confusing idea. Show enough detail for readers to understand the difficulty without turning the post into a list of everything you did.

## What helped

Describe the activity, explanation, feedback, or practice that changed your understanding. Credit people and resources where appropriate. Link only to material you are allowed to share.

## What I understand now

State the lesson in your own words. Explain what evidence changed your mind and what questions remain. Learning about a skill is different from demonstrating proficiency in it.

## My next step

Identify a small, realistic next action. Label it as a plan; do not write as though you have already completed it.

Before publishing, remove all of this guidance and replace it with your reflection. Set `template: false` after you finish.
