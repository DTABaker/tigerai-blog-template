## Workshop checkpoints

- [ ] **25 minutes:** my own starter website opens.
- [ ] I opened Workshop and the studio on my own live site, using my username in the address.
- [ ] **40 minutes:** my name and accurate introduction appear on it.
- [ ] **60 minutes:** one real post is published and findable in Writing.
- [ ] **75 minutes:** About and Résumé describe me accurately; unused sections are removed.
- [ ] **85 minutes:** a partner and I have reviewed the site.
- [ ] **90 minutes:** I have saved the live URL and chosen my next improvement.

## Launch checklist

### Content

- [ ] The homepage uses my name, initials, actual focus, and introduction.
- [ ] About and Résumé contain my own text, without instructional paragraphs.
- [ ] Each published post and project has a real title, useful summary, appropriate date, and accurate content.
- [ ] Unused sample posts and projects still have `template: true`; launch mode will hide them. Their source files may remain for reference.
- [ ] My published items have `draft: false` and `template: false`.
- [ ] Sample dates, `Template` tags, “Your Name,” and “replace this” instructions are gone from my professional pages.
- [ ] I can explain and support the credentials, roles, outcomes, and numbers I mention.
- [ ] Collaborators and sources are credited; plans are clearly labeled as plans.

### The reader's experience

- [ ] I opened the live homepage, Writing, About, Résumé, and any project pages.
- [ ] I searched a word from my post's title, summary, or topic labels, tried a topic filter, then cleared them. Search does not cover article bodies.
- [ ] Every link I added opens the intended destination.
- [ ] Every image loads and has meaningful alternative text.
- [ ] An optional résumé PDF opens and contains the version I intend to share.
- [ ] I checked the site on a phone or a narrow browser window.
- [ ] Text remains readable with my chosen accent color and at a larger browser zoom.

### Publication

- [ ] I reviewed public files for information I do not want online.
- [ ] I set `templateMode: false` in `content/site.yml` after completing the content checks.
- [ ] The latest **Deploy blog** run succeeded.
- [ ] I refreshed the live site and confirmed my own post remains visible while sample cards are hidden.
- [ ] I checked the homepage, About, and Résumé again; launch mode does not replace their instructional text.
- [ ] I copied the website URL, not the GitHub repository URL, for my résumé.

## Partner review

**What I understand about this person's focus after reading the homepage:**  
Write one sentence.

**One place I want more context or evidence:**  
Name the page and the specific passage.

**One link, image, or layout issue to fix:**  
Record a concrete issue, or write “none found in this review.”

## My next improvement

Choose one small action and a realistic time to do it. A thoughtful second post or a clearer project explanation is a useful next step; a large content schedule is optional. If the session ended before your profile text and checklist were ready, keep workshop mode on and finish those before adding the link to your résumé. Deleting unused sample source files is optional cleanup for later.
